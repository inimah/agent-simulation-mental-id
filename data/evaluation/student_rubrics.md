# Part A — Simulated Student Evaluation

**Scoring scale**

| Score | Label     | Meaning |
|------:|-----------|---------|
| 7     | Excellent  | Could not be improved; expert counsellors would fully endorse |
| 6     | Very Good  | Strong with only very minor gaps |
| 5     | Good       | Clearly above average; small but non-critical weaknesses |
| 4     | Adequate   | Meets minimum expectations; noticeable gaps that do not block utility |
| 3     | Weak       | Below expectations; gaps meaningfully reduce training value |
| 2     | Poor       | Significant flaws that impede training utility |
| 1     | Failed     | Entirely inadequate; unusable or actively harmful for training |

---

## Dimension 1 — Authenticity

How authentic does the simulated Indonesian high school student feel as a real person seeking mental health support?

- **7 — Excellent:** Natural Indonesian colloquial speech (gue/lo or aku/kamu). Distress expressed obliquely — the way real teenagers do ("ga tau deh", "biasa aja", "ya gitu"). Age-appropriate inconsistencies, avoidance, and realistic self-awareness limits.
- **5 — Good:** Mostly natural; one or two phrases feel slightly scripted or adult-sounding.
- **3 — Weak:** Frequently sounds rehearsed. Emotional expressions too direct or too structured for a real adolescent.
- **1 — Failed:** Completely inauthentic; robotic, unnaturally formal, or inconsistent with any realistic Indonesian teenager.

## Dimension 2 — Stayed in Role

Did the simulated student consistently maintain their assigned persona and scenario throughout the conversation — no character breaks, no backstory contradictions?

- **7 — Excellent:** Persona traits (e.g., pendiam, reactive, skeptical) are visible and consistent across all turns, including when the chatbot shifts topic or applies pressure.
- **5 — Good:** Generally consistent; one minor slip (e.g., briefly too forthcoming for a reserved persona).
- **3 — Weak:** Noticeable inconsistencies in persona maintenance across multiple turns.
- **1 — Failed:** Frequently broke character or contradicted the assigned scenario.

## Dimension 3 — Mirrored Challenging Aspects

Did the simulated student accurately reflect the challenging aspects defined for the scenario across the full conversation arc?

- **7 — Excellent:** All scenario-defined challenging aspects are present and sustained. Creates realistic difficulty for the chatbot without being artificially obstructive.
- **5 — Good:** Most challenging aspects present; one is underplayed or fades mid-conversation.
- **3 — Weak:** Only surface-level challenging aspects visible; deeper traits absent.
- **1 — Failed:** Did not mirror any challenging aspects; student was too compliant or unrealistically easy.

## Dimension 4 — Ready as Training Partner

Is this simulation ready to be used as a training partner for chatbot fine-tuning or novice counsellor practice, based on the full conversation quality?

- **7 — Excellent:** Meaningful, realistic, and safe training experience covering authentic Indonesian adolescent mental health challenges throughout. No harmful or misleading content.
- **5 — Good:** Usable for training; one aspect that would benefit from refinement.
- **3 — Weak:** Has utility but significant gaps that would require supplementary human review.
- **1 — Failed:** Not at all ready; too flawed, harmful, or misleading for training use.

## Dimension 5 — Recommend to Novices

Would you recommend this simulated student to novice chatbot developers or trainee counsellors as a practice partner?

- **7 — Excellent:** Would strongly recommend. Excellent realism, appropriate difficulty calibration, and high educational value for understanding Indonesian adolescent mental health.
- **5 — Good:** Would recommend with minor caveats.
- **3 — Weak:** Would recommend only with supervision or framing notes.
- **1 — Failed:** Would strongly not recommend; the simulation would mislead or harm novice training.
