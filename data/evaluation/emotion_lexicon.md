# Emotion Lexicon

Keywords used to detect emotion valence in conversation turns.

| Valence       | Subcategories |
|---------------|---------------|
| Emosi Negatif | fear, sadness, anger, shame, loneliness, despair, stress, depression, pain, inability |
| Emosi Positif | comfort, calm, relief, happiness, gratitude, pride, hope, love, achievement |
